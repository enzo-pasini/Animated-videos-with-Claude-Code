Free 3D packs for theme kits (scenes plan, session 3: docs/prompts/3-theme-kits.md)
One line per pack: link | theme | license (if you saw it)
Where to look (low-poly, free):
  kenney.nl        - all CC0, clean low-poly (city, furniture, nature, castles), closest to our style
  quaternius.com   - all CC0, nature, medieval, buildings
  poly.pizza       - search engine for low-poly models, CC0 or CC-BY (check each)
  sketchfab.com    - huge, mixed quality; filter "Downloadable" + license
Licenses: CC0 = free, no credit · CC-BY = free, credit in the video description · non-commercial / personal = do NOT use
Format: .glb / .gltf best; .fbx / .obj also work.
Example:
https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit | home interiors (kitchen, bathroom) | CC0
