# The Job Nobody Wants in Japan
format: explainer · target: 45 s · pov: third · hook: strange-act · you-see: late · ending: button

## Script
[hook] Every morning, a cook named Rosa cut forty onions and cried like a movie ending. [story]
[escalation] So she tried everything. Swimming goggles. A lit candle next to the cutting board. Cutting under running water.
Nothing worked, and the other cooks started calling her station the crying corner.
So Rosa got stubborn. She switched to an old, dull knife to go slower, and the tears got even worse. [F3]
It was impossible to get fired in that kitchen, so she stayed.
Her boss told her to just wear dark sunglasses and stop complaining.
[turn] But one hot afternoon, she grabbed a bag of onions straight from the fridge, and for the first time in years, her eyes stayed completely dry. [F4]
[you-see] You see, an onion is a tiny chemical trap. [F2]
Its cells keep a sulfur compound and an enzyme apart. [F2]
[reveal] When a knife breaks those cells, they mix into a gas that drifts up and stings your eyes. [F2]
Cold slows that reaction down. [F4]
And a sharp knife breaks fewer cells. [F3]
[ending] Rosa still cuts forty onions a day. [story] She just keeps them cold now.
