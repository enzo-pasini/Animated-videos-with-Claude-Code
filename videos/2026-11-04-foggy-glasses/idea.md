# Idea — Mira's foggy glasses (pipeline test, step 13)

PIPELINE TEST: a ~12 s mini explainer to run every step of the flow once (A1–A4). Not for upload.

- **Strange hook:** Mira walks from the cold night into a warm workshop and "goes blind".
- **Odd concrete detail:** her glasses turn white in about a second.
- **Real mechanism:** the lenses are cold from outside; the warm indoor air that touches them cools below
  its dew point, so its water vapor condenses into tiny droplets on the glass (fog).
- **Places:** `night_street` (cold, outside) → `workshop` (warm, inside). Two sets, matches the story.
- **Hook type:** strange-act · **POV:** third (Mira) · **you see:** late · **ending:** button
- **Main character:** Mira (new cast, D9; her silhouette item is the round glasses — the story is about them).
