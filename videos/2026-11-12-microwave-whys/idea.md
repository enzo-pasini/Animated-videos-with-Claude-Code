# Idea — how a microwave oven works (from brief.md)

Topic: how a microwave oven heats food, told as a chain of "why" questions (see `brief.md`).

- Hook: hot soup in a bowl that is barely warm (result first).
- Odd detail: water molecules flip back and forth about 2.45 billion times a second.
- Mechanism: the waves make the polar water molecules twist to follow the field; they bump into their neighbours, and that motion is heat. Glass and ceramic let the waves pass, so the bowl only warms from the soup.
